<article class="intel-container">
  <header>
    <h1 class="intel-header">Ocean Park: South Surrey's Walkable Beach Village</h1>
    <p class="intel-sub">The only neighbourhood in South Surrey where you can walk to the beach, cafes, and boutiques without getting in your car.</p>
  </header>

  <section>
    <p>Ocean Park is South Surrey's coastal village. It's low-density, geographically constrained by the bluffs and ocean, and there's no more land to build on. That creates scarcity. Scarcity creates value.</p>
    <p>This isn't a sprawling suburban neighbourhood. It's a 100-year-old seaside community with a village core on 128th Street. You can walk to coffee, restaurants, the beach, and Kwomais Point Park. That walkability is rare in South Surrey, and people pay a premium for it.</p>

    <h2 class="section-title">What You're Actually Buying</h2>
    <ul class="metric-list">
        <li><strong>Location:</strong> South Surrey, bordered by Boundary Bay to the south and west. 5 minutes to White Rock, 25 minutes to the US border, under an hour to downtown Vancouver.</li>
        <li><strong>Home Types:</strong> A mix of character homes (original 1950s-1980s coastal builds, many renovated) and newer custom village homes. Typical size: 2,400-3,000 square feet.</li>
        <li><strong>Lot Sizes:</strong> Smaller than Elgin Chantrell or Panorama Ridge. Most lots are 6,000-8,000 square feet. You're not getting acreage here. You're getting village proximity.</li>
        <li><strong>The Village Premium:</strong> Homes within walking distance (10-15 minutes) of the 128th Street village core typically command a 10-15% premium over comparable South Surrey homes without that walkability.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2 class="section-title">Why Ocean Park Holds Value</h2>
    <ul class="metric-list">
        <li><strong>Geographic Constraints:</strong> The neighbourhood is bordered by ocean bluffs to the south and west. There's nowhere left to build. No new subdivisions. No more lots. The supply is capped.</li>
        <li><strong>Low Turnover:</strong> Ocean Park doesn't have a lot of inventory turnover. People buy here and stay. When homes do come up for sale, they typically sell within 45-60 days, even in balanced markets.</li>
        <li><strong>Sale to List Price Ratio:</strong> Around 95-96%. Buyers aren't getting massive discounts here. Sellers know the value, and buyers accept it because there aren't many alternatives.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2 class="section-title">The Downsizer Draw</h2>
    <p>Ocean Park is attracting a specific buyer: established professionals and retirees downsizing from larger homes in Elgin Chantrell or Panorama Ridge.</p>
    <p><strong>What they're trading:</strong></p>
    <ul class="metric-list">
        <li>5,000+ square foot homes for 2,400-3,000 square feet</li>
        <li>Large yard maintenance for smaller, manageable lots</li>
        <li>Car dependency for walkability</li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>What they're gaining:</strong></p>
    <ul class="metric-list">
        <li>Walk to coffee, restaurants, and boutiques</li>
        <li>Ocean proximity and beach access</li>
        <li>Village community feel</li>
        <li>Less time on property maintenance, more time on lifestyle</li>
    </ul>
    <p>This trend has held for the last 5+ years and shows no sign of reversing. As Baby Boomers continue to downsize, Ocean Park remains a top destination.</p>

    <h2 class="section-title">Who Ocean Park Is For</h2>
    <p><strong>This area fits you if:</strong></p>
    <ul class="metric-list">
        <li>You want to walk to coffee, the beach, and local shops without driving</li>
        <li>You're downsizing from a larger home and prioritize lifestyle over square footage</li>
        <li>You value coastal proximity and ocean views</li>
        <li>You're okay with smaller lots and character homes (some need updates)</li>
        <li>You want a community feel, not suburban isolation</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>This area does NOT fit if:</strong></p>
    <ul class="metric-list">
        <li>You need 4,000+ square feet and large yard space (see Elgin Chantrell)</li>
        <li>You want brand-new, move-in-ready executive homes (see Morgan Creek)</li>
        <li>You're looking for acreage and privacy (see Panorama Ridge)</li>
        <li>You're car-dependent and don't care about walkability</li>
        <li>You're expecting budget pricing (Ocean Park has decoupled from starter-home affordability)</li>
    </ul>

    <h2 class="section-title">Ocean Park vs The Competition</h2>
    <ul class="metric-list">
        <li><strong>vs Morgan Creek:</strong> Morgan Creek gives you golf course views and lock-and-go strata living. Ocean Park gives you beach village walkability and ocean proximity. Morgan Creek is inland convenience. Ocean Park is coastal lifestyle.</li>
        <li><strong>vs Elgin Chantrell:</strong> Elgin Chantrell gives you larger homes (4,500+ sq ft), bigger lots, and top school catchment. Ocean Park gives you walkability, ocean access, and village character. Elgin Chantrell is for families with kids. Ocean Park is for downsizers and lifestyle buyers.</li>
        <li><strong>vs Panorama Ridge:</strong> Panorama Ridge gives you multi-acre privacy and estate living. Ocean Park gives you village community and walkability. Panorama Ridge is for people who want space. Ocean Park is for people who want proximity.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2 class="section-title">The Resale Reality</h2>
    <p>Ocean Park holds value because you can't recreate it. There's no new land. You can't build another 100-year-old seaside village 5 minutes from White Rock.</p>
    <p>The village walkability and ocean proximity are permanent features. As long as people value walkable communities and coastal access, Ocean Park will maintain its premium.</p>
    <p>Homes here sell faster than most character home markets because the buyer pool is consistent: downsizers, coastal lifestyle seekers, and established professionals who prioritize location over size.</p>
    <p>If you're buying here, you're buying irreplaceability. When you need to sell, there will be buyers looking for exactly what Ocean Park offers.</p>
  </section>
