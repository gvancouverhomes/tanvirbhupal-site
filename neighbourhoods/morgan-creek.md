      <h3 style="color: #1a1a1a; margin-top: 40px; font-weight: normal; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 2px; font-size: 1rem;">Community History & Boundaries</h3>
      <p>The Morgan Creek neighbourhood originally started around 1994 as an executive subdivision surrounding the award-winning Morgan Creek Golf Course. The community's boundaries span 40th Avenue to the North, 32nd Avenue to the South, Highway 99 to the West, and 176th Street (Highway 15) to the East. This meticulously groomed enclave was envisioned with larger lots and high-end homes to match the caliber of the golf facilities.</p>

      <h3 style="color: #1a1a1a; margin-top: 40px; font-weight: normal; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 2px; font-size: 1rem;">Educational Infrastructure</h3>
      <p>Families are drawn to the area for its top-tier educational options. Public elementary students typically attend <strong>Morgan Elementary</strong> or <strong>Rosemary Heights Elementary</strong>, both known for strong academic performance. The prestigious independent <strong>Southridge School</strong> (K-12) is located right in the heart of the community and serves as a major driver for local property values.</p>

      <h3 style="color: #1a1a1a; margin-top: 40px; font-weight: normal; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 2px; font-size: 1rem;">Lifestyle & Amenities</h3>
      <ul style="list-style: none; padding: 0;">
        <li style="padding: 15px 0; border-bottom: 1px solid #eee;"><strong>Golf & Recreation:</strong> The centerpiece is the <strong>Morgan Creek Golf Course</strong>, designed by Thomas McBroom and named BC "Facility of the Year." Local parks like Rosemary Heights Linear Park and Morgan Creek Park provide walking trails and green spaces.</li>
        <li style="padding: 15px 0; border-bottom: 1px solid #eee;"><strong>Retail & Dining:</strong> Just minutes away are the <strong>Grandview Corners</strong> and <strong>The Shops at Morgan Crossing</strong>, offering retailers like Save-On-Foods and Indigo, alongside fine dining favorites like The Keg and Cactus Club.</li>
        <li style="padding: 15px 0; border-bottom: 1px solid #eee;"><strong>Strategic Transit:</strong> Excellent connectivity via Highway 99 and King George Boulevard provides quick access to downtown Vancouver, the US Border, and the rest of the Lower Mainland.</li>
      </ul>

      <h3 style="color: #1a1a1a; margin-top: 40px; font-weight: normal; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 2px; font-size: 1rem;">Market Dynamics</h3>
      <p>While single-family custom homes on generous lots are the dominant housing type, the area has expanded to include high-end townhouses and gated communities like <em>Wedgewood</em>. Current development is introducing the first apartment components near the 10th hole, providing sophisticated options for downsizers who wish to remain within the community's quiet, secure environment.</p>
