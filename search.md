# Beyond the public database.

It is a common misconception that the website you use defines the quality of your search. In British Columbia, almost every platform pulls from the same MLS® data. 

The difference isn't the **data**; it’s the **discretion** and the **strategy** behind it.

### Protect Your Negotiation Power
Many families believe that talking to multiple agents will help them find the "best deal." In reality, this often compromises your leverage before you even step into a home.

- **Leaked Leverage:** Sharing your budget, motives, or timeline with multiple parties broadcasts your "playbook" to an interconnected local network. If a listing agent knows you are in a time crunch or lack other choices, you will likely pay more.
- **Agent Hounding:** Public portals often prioritize volume over your privacy. My process is designed to shield you from the "noise" and keep your intentions confidential.
- **One Expert, One Vision:** By choosing a single advisor, you ensure your strategy remains protected and your interests are guarded by a loyal fiduciary.

---

### The 70% Filter: Removing the Wrong Options
Good advisory work isn’t about maximizing options; it’s about removing the wrong ones early and decisively. Out of 100 listings that fit your criteria "on paper," I typically discard **60% to 70%** immediately before we ever talk about a showing.

I filter for the "Micro-Location Friction" that photos won't show:
* **Infrastructure Creep:** Identifying busy collector roads or awkward street layouts with cut-through traffic.
* **Density Risk:** Reviewing Neighborhood Concept Plan (NCP) amendments that threaten a street's long-term single-family character.
* **Staging Masks:** Recognizing build-era patterns or developer histories where fundamentals may have been compromised.

---

### The Hidden Inventory: Private Opportunities
While the public MLS® is the primary tool, it is not the only one. In the executive markets of Surrey and South Surrey, a significant subset of opportunities occurs away from the public eye. 

These "off-market" or discreet sales are often driven by privacy mandates for high-profile owners, or represent homes currently being prepared for sale and only available to a small network of trusted local advisors. My search process includes monitoring these private channels to ensure you have a perspective that isn't limited to a public search bar.
